Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal and farm data

Introduction

FarmFinances ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and disclose your information, including personal information, in conjunction with your access to and use of the FarmFinances platform.

By using FarmFinances, you understand and agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy whenever you access the platform to stay informed about our information practices and your privacy rights.

Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services. The types of information we may collect include:

1. Personal Information

  • Account Information: When you register for a FarmFinances account, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and basic demographic information.
  • Financial Information: To provide credit evaluation services, we may collect financial information including bank account details, existing loan information, and transaction history.
  • Identity Verification Information: We may collect government-issued identification and other information to verify your identity and comply with regulatory requirements.

2. Farm Data

  • Agricultural Information: We collect information about your farming operations, including farm size, crop types, yield history, and farming practices.
  • Geographical Information: We collect location data, including GIS data, property boundaries, and geographical coordinates of your agricultural land.
  • Environmental Data: We collect soil health metrics, weather patterns, and other environmental factors relevant to your agricultural operations.

3. Usage and Technical Information

  • Device Information: We collect information about the devices you use to access our services, including hardware model, operating system, IP address, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about your interactions with the FarmFinances platform, including pages visited, features used, and time spent on the platform.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect information about your browsing activities and to personalize your experience on our platform.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including:

1. Providing and Improving Our Services

  • Generating credit evaluations based on agricultural and financial data
  • Connecting farmers with appropriate financial institutions
  • Developing new products, services, and features
  • Analyzing and improving the performance and functionality of our platform

2. Communication

  • Sending you service-related communications
  • Providing customer support and responding to your inquiries
  • Sending promotional messages and marketing communications when permitted
  • Notifying you about changes to our services, terms, or policies

3. Legal and Security Purposes

  • Complying with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes
  • Enforcing our terms, conditions, and policies
  • Detecting, investigating, and preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activities
  • Protecting the rights, property, or safety of FarmFinances, our users, or others

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information in the following situations:

1. With Your Consent

We share your information with financial institutions and lenders only with your explicit consent. You can control which institutions have access to your credit evaluation and farm data through your account settings.

2. Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party service providers to perform functions and provide services to us, such as hosting services, data analysis, payment processing, and customer service. These providers have access to your information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

3. Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).

4. Business Transfers

If FarmFinances is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of its assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice on our platform of any such change in ownership or use of your information.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the security of your personal information. However, please understand that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access and obtain a copy of your personal information
  • The right to rectify any inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • The right to erase your personal information
  • The right to restrict the processing of your personal information
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to object to the processing of your personal information
  • The right to withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@farmfinances.com.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
