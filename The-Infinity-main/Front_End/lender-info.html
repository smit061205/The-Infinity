Lender Information

Partner with FarmFinances to offer better financial products to farmers

Why Partner With FarmFinances?

As a financial institution, partnering with FarmFinances provides you with access to our proprietary agricultural credit evaluation system that goes beyond traditional credit scores. By incorporating agricultural data, soil health metrics, and GIS analysis, we help you make more informed lending decisions for farmers.

Reduce Default Risk

Our alternative credit evaluation helps you identify promising farming operations that might be overlooked by traditional credit scoring systems.

Expand Your Customer Base

Reach previously underserved farmers with appropriate financial products based on accurate risk assessment.

Streamlined Documentation

Access comprehensive reports that explain credit evaluations, supporting your regulatory compliance and internal review processes.

Customized Loan Products

Design agricultural loan products that better align with seasonal cash flow and specific farming operation types.

How It Works for Lenders

1

Establish Partnership

Complete our partnership agreement and integrate with our secure API system.

2

Receive Credit Evaluations

3

Offer Customized Financing

4

Monitor Performance

Track loan performance with ongoing updates to agricultural data and credit profiles.
